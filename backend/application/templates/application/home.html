About CallYourMomma

At CallYourMomma, we believe flavor should be bold, authentic, and just a little dangerous. We handcraft hot sauces using farm-fresh peppers, old-school cooking techniques, and recipes passed down—and turned up—through generations.

CallYourMomma started right out of a family kitchen in Hayward, California—just a few jars sold to friends, neighbors, and anyone who could handle the heat. What began as homemade gifts quickly turned into a passion project. Every bottle carries the same love and fire we first shared around the dinner table.
